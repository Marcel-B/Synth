Instruments

14 plugins

Software instruments for an instrument track. Each comes with its own factory sounds, no copies from factory ROMs or other people's preset banks, all matched in loudness. In Logic under Instrument → AU Instruments → b-velop.

Tonwerk Analog

aumu TwAn Bvlp

The analog synth from Tonwerk in the browser, as a plugin. Sounds from Tonwerk sound the same here as there.

  • Two oscillators with pulse and PWM, plus noise
  • Lowpass, highpass and bandpass with their own envelope
  • Wavefolder and sample & hold, LFO also in song tempo
  • Delay and reverb built in, 16 voices
SubtractivePoly

Tonwerk FM

aumu TwFm Bvlp

Four sine operators in eight algorithms, like the 4-operator FM synths of the eighties and identical to Tonwerk's FM synth.

  • An envelope and velocity per operator
  • Feedback on operator 4
  • LFO on pitch, index or volume
  • Electric pianos, bells, basses and brass as factory sounds
FMPoly

Tonwerk DX

aumu TwDx Bvlp

Six operators and 32 algorithms for the classic FM sound of the eighties: electric piano, bells, brass and basses.

  • Envelopes and levels in the original's units, 0 to 99
  • LFO speed matched against measurements of real hardware
  • Reads SysEx banks of classic 6-operator synths
  • A full bank of 32 sounds of its own
FM6 operators

Tonwerk Wavetable

aumu TwWt Bvlp

A wavetable synth for dubstep: wobble, growl, reese, riddim, screeches and wide leads.

  • Two oscillators with warp, unison and sub
  • Tables from formulas, nothing copied from other synths
  • Modulation matrix with eight slots and four macros
  • 33 factory sounds, the basses equally loud for the drop
WavetableDubstep

Tonwerk Granular

aumu TwGr Bvlp

Cuts a sound into many short grains and puts them back together as pads, clouds and frozen moments.

  • Up to 40 grains per voice, 5 ms to 1 s long
  • Nine built-in sources or a file of your own
  • Position, spray, scan, reverse and spread across the stereo field
  • Equally loud with few grains and with many
GranularTextures

Tonwerk Physical

aumu TwPh Bvlp

Computes how an instrument vibrates: plucked, struck, bowed or blown, on a string, tube, bar, bell, membrane or bowl.

  • Exciter, resonator and body combine freely
  • Every hit sounds a little different
  • Bow and breath sustain the note as long as the key is down
  • Waveguides and modal resonators, computed sparingly
Physical modelingAcoustic

Tonwerk Additiv

aumu TwAd Bvlp

Builds sounds from up to 64 single partials, clean and glassy, for bells, organs, choirs and living pads.

  • Two spectra, morphed into each other seamlessly
  • Vowel formants, comb, tilt and stretch
  • An envelope of its own for every partial, if you like
  • Cannot alias, eight partials per vector instruction
AdditivePoly

Tonwerk PD

aumu TwPd Bvlp

Phase distortion as in the eighties: a cosine wave with a bent phase, digital and still warm.

  • Eight waves from saw to resonant sweep
  • Two lines with their own DCW and DCA envelopes
  • Ring and noise, stereo chorus
  • Needs no filter and hardly any CPU
Phase distortion80s

Tonwerk Sampler

aumu TwSm Bvlp

A sampler for your own recordings: chromatic with a loop or cut into slices, one key per piece.

  • Slices on a grid or at the transients
  • Forward, ping-pong and reverse loops with crossfade
  • Filter, LFO and lo-fi
  • Nine built-in sounds to get started
SamplerSlices

Tonwerk Bass

aumu TwBs Bvlp

An acid bass: one voice, a squelching lowpass, slide and accent.

  • Slide only while a key is held, without a new attack
  • Accents in a row build the sweep up
  • Ladder filter with drive, full resonance stays bounded
  • Modelled on the classics, nothing copied
AcidMono

Tonwerk Neuro

aumu TwNr Bvlp

A monophonic bass for the reese and neuro sounds of drum and bass and dubstep: beating saws, dirty FM and a clean sub.

  • Reese from two detuned saws, the sub bypassing drive and filter
  • FM pair with feedback and bite from the mod envelope
  • Ladder, bandpass, notch or comb, drive up to 30 dB
  • Wobble LFO in song tempo, glide and legato
BassMono

Tonwerk Expander

aumu TwEx Bvlp

An eight-voice analog synth in the manner of the expander modules of the seventies: two oscillators and a 12 dB filter that sweeps seamlessly from lowpass through notch to highpass.

  • Saw and pulse with PWM, hard sync with an envelope sweep
  • Lowpass, notch, highpass seamlessly, bandpass switch
  • Poly, mono or all eight voices in unison
  • Drift: every voice a little different, like separate modules
  • Hardware sheet: every sound as a picture with the knob positions for the real module
PolyAnalog

Tonwerk Groovebox

aumu TwGb Bvlp

A drum machine with eight synthesised instruments and a step sequencer in time with the song.

  • Kick, snare, clap, two toms, hi-hats and cymbal
  • A machine of its own per track, in the style of classic drum computers
  • 13 knobs per track, swing and accents
  • Dice: chance in the pattern, for all tracks or per track
  • Delay and reverb with sends per track, switched off with a click
  • Separate outputs per track (multi-output)
  • No samples: every knob acts seamlessly
DrumsSequencer

Tonwerk Rig

aumu TwRg Bvlp

One instrument with up to six effects behind it, saved as one sound.

  • Any Tonwerk instrument, followed by Delay, Shimmer, Tape, Rumble and all the other effects
  • With the Groovebox, an effect on one instrument only, such as Rumble on the kick
  • The plugins' own interfaces, with their presets
  • Latency compensated, also between the Groovebox's tracks
  • Playable in the iPad app: that is how the effects get there too

Effects

17 plugins

Audio effects for any track and any bus, with level-matched presets. In Logic under Audio FX → Audio Units → b-velop; Robovox and Vocoder go into the instrument slot and hear the voice through the side chain, so that they get MIDI.

Chrome Glitch

aufx ChGl Bvlp

For a vocal track whose speech chrome is failing: syllables stutter, the voice drops out, crumbles and jumps in pitch.

  • Stutters, dropouts and pitch jumps in song tempo
  • Crusher, full during a glitch and light in between
  • The same glitch at the same place in the song on every bounce
  • Monitor shows CHROME OK, INTERFERENCE, NO SIGNAL
GlitchVocals

Tonwerk Distortion

aufx TwDs Bvlp

The circuit of a classic guitar distortion pedal, computed stage by stage, plus tube, clipper, folder, rectifier and bitcrusher.

  • Diode clipper from the Shockley equation
  • Four times oversampled with ADAA, little aliasing
  • Dry/wet without comb filtering
  • Presets as loud as the dry signal
DistortionPedal

Tonwerk Robovox

aumf TwRv Bvlp

The robot voice of AI songs: a low, steady synth sings with the formants of your voice.

  • Carrier from the voice's pitch, snapped to a scale
  • Or played by MIDI, through the side chain
  • Formant, bass and sibilance adjustable
  • STFT vocoder with fine resolution
VocoderVocals

Tonwerk Vocoder

aumf TwVc Bvlp

The classic vocoder: your voice shapes a synth that you play on the keyboard, chords included.

  • Its own eight-voice synth as the carrier
  • 8 to 32 bands from 100 Hz to 8 kHz
  • Formant, freeze, sibilance and stereo width
  • A single note sounds as loud as a chord
VocoderMIDI

Tonwerk Wobble

aufx TwWb Bvlp

The wobble bass of dubstep on any track: filter and volume, moved by LFO curves you draw yourself.

  • Curves with steps and bends per segment
  • On the beat, wherever playback starts
  • Lowpass, highpass, bandpass and vowel filter
  • Presets with ready-made curves

Freezes a moment of the sound into an endless pad that you keep playing over.

  • No latency, the dry signal keeps running

  • Six LFO shapes with stereo offset

Beat repeat on the song's grid: stutters, fills and rolls that sound the same on every bounce.

  • Grid from 1/4 to 1/64, triplets too

  • Chance and seed, reproducible in the song

Tonwerk Multiband

Three bands, three compressors: pulls loud parts down and brings quiet ones up, all the way to the dense dubstep sound.

  • Linkwitz-Riley crossovers, flat without compression
  • Downward and upward in every band
  • Depth for all bands at once, solo per band
  • Mix without comb filtering at the crossover frequencies

  • Bell, lows or highs, freely adjustable

MIDI effects

2 plugins

They sit before the instrument and play or move it. In Logic in the MIDI FX slot above the instrument: MIDI FX → Audio Units → b-velop.

Tonwerk Arp

aumi TwAr Bvlp

An arpeggiator and step sequencer in front of any synth: you hold keys, it plays notes from them on the grid.

  • 16 steps with pitch, velocity and tie
  • Arpeggiator or sequencer from one pattern
  • The grid sits on the song's bars
  • Works with any instrument, Logic's own included
MIDISequencer

Tonwerk LFO

aumi TwLf Bvlp

An LFO that moves any knob of a Tonwerk instrument: it sends a MIDI controller, and the instrument learns it with a right click on the knob.

  • Six shapes, free or in song tempo
  • 14 bits, so slow sweeps do not step
  • Several in a row for several knobs
  • Starts with the song or with every phrase

Download

All plugins come together in one package. The latest version is on GitHub Releases; to update, download the new package and install it the same way. Imported sounds and saved projects are kept.

macOS

Audio Unit for Logic, VST3 and the apps, for Apple silicon and Intel from macOS 11. Signed and notarized by Apple.

Download the zip, quit Logic, then in Terminal:

cd ~/Downloads/Tonwerk-Synth-*-macOS
zsh install.sh

Linux

VST3 and the apps for x64, for example for Bitwig, Reaper or Ardour. From version 0.5.0. The install.sh of newer releases adds the apps with their icons to the applications menu.

In a terminal:

tar -xzf Tonwerk-Synth-*-Linux-x64.tar.gz
cd Tonwerk-Synth-*-Linux-x64
./install.sh

Windows

VST3 and the apps for x64. From version 0.5.0. Not signed, so Windows asks the first time an app starts.

Unpack the zip and copy the contents of the VST3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, where every DAW finds them.

iPad

One app, Tonwerk, with every plugin as an AUv3 for Logic Pro for iPad, GarageBand and AUM. On its own it plays the instruments with a large keyboard and a MIDI keyboard, with Tonwerk Rig also with effects behind them, and shows in the scope what arrives at the microphone. The interfaces adapt to the screen, and a long press on a knob opens MIDI learn.

iPadOS 15 or later, in the App Store.

Eurorack module

Tonwerk Wavetable to build yourself

The sound engine of Tonwerk Wavetable on an ESP32-S3, as a 14 HP Eurorack module: two wavetable oscillators with warp, unison and sub, a multimode filter with drive, an envelope, V/Oct, gate and four CV inputs. Through-hole boards, front panel and knobs from the 3D printer, firmware flashed in the browser.

Tonwerk-AI is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that makes songs with the music models YuE2 and ACE-Step on your own Mac, plays them and opens them as a Logic project. The two synths Analog and FM play the tracks there.

Source code

Our own code

Every sound is computed, not played back: wavetables from formulas, drums from oscillators and noise, instruments from their physics. Classic synthesizers, drum machines and pedals are modelled, none of them copied.

The instruments Analog and FM are the two browser synths from Tonwerk; a sound saved there can be imported and sounds the same here as there.
