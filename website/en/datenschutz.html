The Tonwerk app and the plugins

The Tonwerk app (iPad and Mac) and the plugins collect no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reports sent to me. What you set, save and load (presets, your own samples, SysEx banks, settings) stays on your device, in the app or in the project of your music app.

Microphone: Tonwerk Scope and the effects listen to the input you choose, in order to display or process it. The signal is processed only on the device, neither recorded nor transmitted.

Network: Only if you yourself enter the address of a Tonwerk server in Tonwerk Analog, FM or Synth and fetch presets does the plugin connect to that server. It belongs to you or to whoever's address you entered; I receive nothing from it.

App Store: Purchase, download and updates run through Apple, and Apple's privacy policy applies to them. If you have agreed in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides me with anonymised crash and usage statistics; you can turn this off there at any time.

Tonwerk-AI

For the app Tonwerk-AI (iPhone, iPad and Mac), which makes songs on your own server, a separate privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of the processing of your data, to object and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As this site itself stores no data, please contact GitHub or me (marcel@tonwerk.studio) about the server logs.
