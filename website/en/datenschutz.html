Privacy

A translation for convenience; the German version is the binding one.

In short

This site sets no cookies, counts no visitors and loads nothing from third parties, not even fonts. There is no contact form and no account.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When a page is requested, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as the IP address, date and time, the page requested and the browser, to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is a secure, reachable website. GitHub may process these data in the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details are in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Downloads and links to GitHub

The downloads and the source code are on github.com. Following a link there leaves this site; GitHub’s privacy statement applies then.

The Tonwerk app and the plugins

The Tonwerk app (iPad and Mac) and the plugins collect no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reports to me. What you set, save and load (presets, your own samples, SysEx banks, settings) stays on your device, in the app or in your music app’s project.

Microphone: Tonwerk Scope and the effects listen to the input you choose, to show it or to process it. The signal is processed on the device only, neither recorded nor transmitted.

Network: Only when you yourself enter the address of a Tonwerk server in Tonwerk Analog, FM or Synth and fetch presets does the plugin connect to that server. It belongs to you or to whoever’s address you entered; I receive nothing from it.

App Store: Purchase, download and updates go through Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy. If you agreed in your device’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives me anonymised crash and usage statistics; you can switch that off there at any time.

Tonwerk-AI

The Tonwerk-AI app (iPhone, iPad and Mac), which makes songs on your own server, has a privacy policy of its own.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of the processing of your data, to object and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As this site itself stores no data, turn to GitHub or to me (marcel@tonwerk.studio) about the server logs.
