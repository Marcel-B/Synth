Tonwerk Physical as a Eurorack module

A second firmware for the same boards as the wavetable module: the sound engine of the plugin Tonwerk Physical. Instead of playing back waveforms, the module computes how an instrument vibrates. An exciter (pluck, strike, bow, blow) sets a resonator (string, tube, bar, bell, skin, bowl) ringing, optionally through a filter and a body.

Overview

  • The same engine as the plugin, rewritten in float: string and tubes as delay lines, bar, bell, skin and bowl as up to 16 resonances. A test on the computer compares all 28 pairs of exciter and resonator with the plugin.
  • Up to four voices: each strike takes the next one, the old ones ring out. A sequence of notes becomes a chord or a harp.
  • Two outputs: ROH (raw) is the voices without the body, KLANG (sound) the same through the body (guitar, violin, box, soundboard). Without a body both are the same.
  • Six inputs: V/OCT, GATE and CV for hardness, position, force and damping.
  • Its own front panel, meant to be yellow with black lettering, so the two modules are easy to tell apart in the rack.

This firmware is tested on the computer; it has never run on an ESP32 yet. I estimate the load at 10–30 % of a core.

Operation

The front is the same as on the wavetable module, only the lettering differs. The WARP button is called ERREGER (exciter) here, the FILTER button RESON. (resonator). Layers, pick-up, holding versus tapping, saving and calibration work just as described there. The panel's labels are German; the tables give their meaning.

Striking

With a gate
Every rising edge at GATE plucks or strikes the next voice, as hard as KRAFT is set. When the gate falls, the damper comes down on the newest voice, as fast as DECAY in the RESON. layer says. By default the damper is off (60 s), every note rings out.
With V/OCT only
While the gate is on, or no cable is in GATE, every jump at V/OCT of at least half a semitone strikes. A sequencer without a gate thus plays every new note; a glide or a slow LFO only moves the pitch.
Pitch
The newest voice follows V/OCT, TUNE and FINE; the older ones ring on at the pitch they were struck at.
Bow and blow
Hold the note as long as the gate is on; their envelope is in the ERREGER layer. Without a gate cable the bow plays all the time: a drone shaped with KRAFT, POSITION and DÄMPF.

First patches

Harp from a sequencer
Pitch into V/OCT, no gate cable, KLANG to the mixer. Pluck on the string; ERREGER layer: four voices (DECAY fully right); RESON. layer: body guitar. DECAY long in the normal layer.
Marimba
Strike on the bar, HÄRTE in the middle, DECAY short, gate from the sequencer. A random CV at POS makes every hit a little different.
Bowed drone
Bow on the string or the bowl, no gate cable. A slow LFO at KRAFT, a second one at POS; DÄMPF. shapes the brightness.
Bells
Strike on the bell, DECAY long, INHARM. turned up a little, two to four voices.

Front panel

The same three variants as for the wavetable module (build guide, step 2), with this firmware's lettering: raised lettering, engraved lettering and inlaid lettering (3MF, two colours). Meant to be yellow with black lettering: for the raised print start with yellow and change to black at 2.5 mm.

Firmware

The firmware is one file, tonwerk-physical.bin, written to address 0x0 as in the build guide, step 8. It needs no tables; the first start takes no longer than any other. Going back to the wavetable firmware works the same way, with tonwerk-modul.bin.

Flashing erases the saved settings and the calibration. So calibrate again after flashing.
