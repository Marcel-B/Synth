Privacy

This policy applies to the app Tonwerk-AI for iPhone, iPad and Mac (bundle ID de.bvelop.tonwerk.ai) and to the server it works with. For this website, the privacy policy of tonwerk.studio applies.

This is a translation for convenience. The German Datenschutzerklärung is the legally binding version.

In short

Tonwerk-AI collects no data for me. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics; the app contains no advertising, tracking or analytics libraries. The app talks only to the server whose address you enter yourself, and that runs on your own Mac; the Mac app also asks GitHub for updates (see below). I receive nothing from any of it.

Controller

Marcel Benders, address in the legal notice, email marcel@tonwerk.studio.

What the app processes

Everything you create or choose in the app goes to your server and is stored there: style, lyrics, scores, ratings, notes, playlists, photos for covers or lyrics drafts, recordings and files you upload. The app loads songs, covers and the state of the queue from there. The connection runs over HTTPS, on the road through Tailscale's encrypted network.

On the device itself the app remembers only its settings: the server's address and the forms last filled in (in the app settings of iOS or macOS). Files you export or share end up where you save or send them. In demo mode the app talks to no one; the sample songs are in the app, changes only in memory.

Permissions

Local Network
to reach your server when it runs in the same network or on the same Mac.
Microphone
only in the speech lab and on the Singing page, when you record a voice. The recording goes to your server, which stores it as a reference for the speech or singing models.
Bluetooth
to play tracks of the Logic page on Bluetooth MIDI instruments. Only MIDI notes are sent.
Photos and files
through the system's picker: the app sees only the photo or file you choose and sends it to your server.

Your server

You run the server yourself, on your Mac; you are responsible for the data there, not me. It computes with local models, and the songs do not leave the Mac. Only if you set it up does it connect to these services:

Anthropic (Claude)
If an API key of your own from Anthropic is on the server, the following goes to Anthropic PBC (USA): with Draft lyrics using a Claude model, the keywords, the style, a chosen photo and the lyrics; with the Sketch, the song idea and your wishes; when painting a cover, the description, to translate it into English. Anthropic's privacy policy applies; by its own account, Anthropic does not use input through the API for training by default. Without a key nothing goes there; the local language models in LM Studio (also for the sketch and the Write page) stay on the Mac.
Nextcloud
the nightly backup of database and songs, to the Nextcloud whose address you enter.
Downloads
During setup and on first use the server downloads programs and models from their sources (such as GitHub, Homebrew, Hugging Face); these providers see your Mac's address.
Web Push
only for the web interface in the browser: if you turn on notifications there, the messages go through your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla). The app uses no push notifications.
Tailscale
connects your devices; for that Tailscale Inc. (Canada/USA) sees which devices are in your tailnet, not the content of the connection. Tailscale's privacy policy applies.

Download and updates (Mac)

You download the Mac app from GitHub Releases, also through the link on this website. GitHub Inc. (USA) processes your IP address and your browser's information; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and GitHub's privacy statement applies. I see only how often a file was downloaded, not by whom.

The Mac app looks for updates with Sparkle: if you allow it or choose Check for Updates Now in the settings, it loads the list of versions (appcast.xml) from GitHub, on its own at most once a day, and with an update the new DMG. GitHub sees your IP address and the app's version; Sparkle sends no further data about your Mac. Automatic checking can be turned off in the settings. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is a secure, current app.

Apple and TestFlight (iPhone and iPad)

For iPhone and iPad the app is distributed through Apple's TestFlight; Apple's privacy policy applies to it. Apple provides me with the name and email address you were invited with, plus device, system version, installations and crash reports, and the feedback and screenshots you send yourself in TestFlight. I use this only to improve the app and to answer you, and delete it when the test ends or you ask me to. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is a working app. If you have agreed in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives me anonymised crash and usage statistics; you can turn this off there at any time.

Contact by email

If you write to me, I use your address and message only to answer (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them when the matter is settled.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of the processing of your data, to data portability, to object and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia. You can view and delete the data on your server there yourself; deleting the app removes its settings from the device.
